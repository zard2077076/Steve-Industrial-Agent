# Privacy and diagnostics

Diagnostics are local, opt-in and allowlisted. They include product/runtime
versions, Java/OS name, dimension ID, network=false and telemetry=false.
Absolute paths, tokens, chat, playerdata, world seed, coordinates, container
contents and world files are excluded or redacted. The fixed ZIP contains only
`diagnostics.txt`, is never uploaded automatically and may be inspected before
manual attachment to an issue.
