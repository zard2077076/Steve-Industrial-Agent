# Formal World Candidate Zones

The accepted FS-12 sample produced eight deterministic machine-seeded candidate IDs. Exact coordinates, bounds, anchors, score breakdowns, nearby findings and coverage remain only in the ignored `candidate-zones.tsv`; they are not copied into Git-tracked documentation.

Every candidate is negative-scored, `UNKNOWN` confidence and `PENDING_USER_SELECTION`. All require `CLAIM_PERMISSION`, `DRY_RUN_VALIDATION`, `PROTECTED_OR_UNKNOWN_REVIEW`, `REGION_AUTHORIZATION` and `USER_SELECTION`; some additionally require `COVERAGE_COMPLETION`. The common blocking facts are unverified clear space, UNKNOWN claim permission and unresolved player-building boundaries.

The ordering is review-only. No top candidate was auto-selected, no region was approved, and no candidate supplies a deployment capability. A later Stage-B package may preserve multiple pending choices, but cannot issue a valid formal approval without a user-selected exact candidate and new current evidence.
