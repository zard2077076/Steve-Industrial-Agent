# Demolition approval UX

Review & Confirm performs a new server survey and displays goal, quantity,
anchor, orientation, layout, footprint, required inputs, place/reuse/clear
counts, hard conflicts, Bot recommendation, phase/risk labels and the active
safety policy on one page.

Protected, container/data, unknown and hazardous findings disable approval. A
successful token expires after two minutes and binds world, dimension, player,
project, goal, quantity, anchor, orientation, layout, execution mode, region,
policy, mutation budget, plan hash, snapshot hash and every approved obstacle
state. Site drift or reselection invalidates it. Approval authorizes only the
listed light clearing; it is not general construction or container authority.
